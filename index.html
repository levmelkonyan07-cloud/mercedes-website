Modern Masterpieces

Mercedes-AMG G 63

The G-Class is a global icon of luxury and off-road supremacy. Originally engineered for military use, the modern G 63 features a hand-built 4.0L V8 Biturbo engine delivering 577 horsepower. It seamlessly blends its legendary boxy silhouette with cutting-edge technology, including the MBUX widescreen cockpit and superior Nappa leather interiors.

Mercedes-Maybach S-Class

The pinnacle of automotive sophistication, the Maybach S-Class is designed for those who seek the highest level of comfort. With an extended wheelbase for maximum legroom, it offers executive rear seating with massage functions and handcrafted wood trim, featuring a revolutionary Burmester 4D Surround Sound system.

V-Class Exclusive

The V-Class redefines the premium MPV segment, combining the spaciousness of a van with the luxury of an S-Class. It serves as a mobile office or a first-class family lounge, featuring face-to-face seating arrangements and high-end materials, providing a smooth journey for business delegations.

AMG GT Black Series

Born on the racetrack, the AMG GT Black Series is a street-legal masterpiece of aerodynamics. Featuring the most powerful AMG V8 engine ever built, it produces 720 horsepower. Its massive carbon-fiber wing allows it to dominate tracks with a 0-60 mph sprint in just 3.1 seconds.

EQS Electric Sedan

The EQS is the flagship of the electric future, featuring the breathtaking MBUX Hyperscreen—a 56-inch curved glass surface. With an industry-leading drag coefficient of 0.20 and a range exceeding 450 miles, the EQS offers a glimpse into a zero-emission world of elegance.

X-Class Pickup

The X-Class entered the market as the world's first true premium pickup truck. It combines a robust ladder-frame chassis with refined driving dynamics, perfect for owners who require heavy towing and off-road exploration while enjoying a high-tech cabin.

Vision AVTR Concept

Inspired by the movie "Avatar," the Vision AVTR showcases future human-machine interaction. It recognizes the driver’s heartbeat and features bionic flaps that allow it to move sideways, symbolizing a vision where technology and nature live in perfect harmony.

GLE 63 S Coupe

The GLE Coupe is a dynamic fusion of an SUV’s strength and a coupe’s athletic elegance. Its sloping roofline and aggressive AMG body styling create a powerful road presence, while the intelligent 4MATIC+ drive ensures perfect grip and stability.
